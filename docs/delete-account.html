Delete your Destiny account

App: Destiny · Developer: Vishruth Dev · Last updated: 6 September 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Destiny account and all data associated with it. You can do it yourself inside the app, or ask the developer to do it for you.

Deletion is permanent. Your habits, revision topics, streaks, completion history, and settings are erased and cannot be recovered afterwards — not by you, and not by the developer. If you want to keep a record, note it down before you delete.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

This is immediate and needs no one's help.

  1. Open Destiny and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap Settings in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete.
  4. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete again.

Your data is erased and you are returned to the login screen.

If you are asked to sign in again: for security, Google requires a recent sign-in before an account can be removed. Sign in once more, then repeat the steps above and the deletion will complete.

Option 2 — Ask the developer to delete it

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email vdev2492@gmail.com from the email address on your Destiny account, with the subject Delete my account.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. A reply confirms once it is done. The email address is used only to locate your account and confirm the deletion, and is not kept afterwards.

What gets deleted

Everything below is erased permanently:

DataDeleted
Your account: email address, display name, account identifierYes, immediately
All habits, including start dates, times, and completion historyYes, immediately
All revision topics and their progressYes, immediately
Your app settings, including Strict ModeYes, immediately
Notification tokens registered to your devicesYes, immediately
Data stored locally on your phoneRemoved when you uninstall the app

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is retained after deletion. Destiny keeps no backups, archives, or analytics records of deleted accounts, and no copy of your data is held for any retention period.

Deleted records may persist briefly in Google Cloud's routine infrastructure backups before being overwritten on Google's own schedule. This is standard Google Cloud behaviour and is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation. That data is not accessible to the developer.

Deleting without deleting your account

To remove individual items instead of the whole account, use Remove habit on the Habits tab or the equivalent control on the Revisions tab. Deleted items are removed straight away.
