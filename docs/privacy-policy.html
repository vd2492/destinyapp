Privacy Policy for Destiny

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Destiny is a habit and revision tracking app for Android, published by Vishruth Dev. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Destiny stores the habits and revision topics you create, plus the email address and name on your account, so they sync across your devices. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible for your data

Vishruth Dev is the data controller for information collected through Destiny. For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights described below, contact vdev2492@gmail.com.

What Destiny collects

Account information

Collected when you create an account or sign in, and required for the app to work at all.

  • Your email address
  • The display name you choose when registering, or the name on your Google account if you use Google Sign-In
  • An account identifier generated by Firebase Authentication
  • The dates your account was created and last updated

Destiny never sees or stores your password. If you sign in with an email and password, that password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google, Destiny receives only a sign-in token and never your Google password.

Content you create

Everything you enter in the app, stored so it survives reinstalls and syncs between your devices.

  • Habit names, start dates, and the time of day each habit is scheduled
  • Revision topic names, start dates, and revision times
  • Which days you marked complete or in progress, used to calculate streaks and completion rates
  • Whether reminders are switched on for each habit or topic
  • Your Strict Mode preference

Notification information

Collected so reminders can reach your device.

  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging token identifying your app installation
  • Your device model, the platform name, and the app package name, so that a token can be matched to the right device and stale tokens cleaned up

This identifies an app installation, not you personally, and is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

Stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone and is not transmitted anywhere:

  • A local copy of your habits and revision topics, so the app works without a connection
  • The identifiers of reminders currently scheduled with the Android alarm system
  • A flag recording that the app has already asked you once about the alarm permission

Uninstalling Destiny removes all of it.

What Destiny does not collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, files, or microphone access
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting service
  • No third-party trackers or social media SDKs

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing.

Where your data is stored

Destiny uses Google Firebase as its only backend. There is no separate server operated by the developer.

ServiceWhat it holds
Firebase AuthenticationYour email address, display name, and account identifier
Cloud FirestoreYour habits, revision topics, completion history, settings, and notification tokens
Firebase Cloud MessagingThe token used to deliver reminder notifications

Google processes this data on the developer's behalf as a service provider. Google's handling of it is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

How your data is protected

  • All traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS
  • Data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud infrastructure
  • Firestore security rules restrict every document to the account that owns it, so no other user can read or modify your data

No system is perfectly secure, and this policy does not promise otherwise.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your habits, revision topics, completion history, settings, and notification tokens are erased immediately and permanently, along with your authentication record. Deleted data cannot be recovered, including by the developer.

Your rights

  • Access — all data Destiny holds about you is visible inside the app
  • Correction — you can edit or remove any habit or revision topic at any time
  • Deletion — see Delete your account, or email the address above
  • Withdrawing consent — deleting your account withdraws consent for all processing

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the DPDP Act. Contact the address above to exercise them.

Children

Destiny is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact the address above and it will be deleted.
